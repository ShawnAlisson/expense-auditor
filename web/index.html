Expense Auditor

Every line is one claim ("this expense is permitted") checked by Banyan Sapling against the policy clause and the evidence on file. Sapling returns a label and probabilities in one forward pass, with no generation. We act automatically only when the top probability is at least 75%. Below that, the line goes to a person with Sapling's lean attached. Sapling's own calibrated status is shown as-is; it is conservative on this preview model.
